10X Change

An app that remembers what you decided to change, shows you whether you are changing, and makes the next thirty seconds easy.

Most people who read a book about the self finish it, decide to do something, and lose the thread within two weeks. The old loops keep running underneath the new practice until the practice fades into the background. 10X Change is built for that reality. It keeps a map of who you are across seven bodies, lets you change one thing per week, measures the shift, and celebrates every percentage point.

How a week works

  1. Choose one action for the week from your own top three.
  2. Mark last week's action: keep it, let it coast, or let it go.
  3. Answer a ten-second question a few times a day, and do one thirty-second thing when asked.
  4. Each night, tell the app the high and low of the day. It listens and remembers.
  5. On Sunday, see how far you have come, what moved it, and what is likely next.

Your baseline map, in two minutes

Rate where each body stands today. Zero is as far from your possible self as you can imagine; one hundred is already there. Nothing here is sent anywhere; copy the result to keep it.
